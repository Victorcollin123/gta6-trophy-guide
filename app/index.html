    Roadmap to the platinum

    Built from the official trophy descriptions alone: play the story first, then work the map, the races and the robberies, and save the 100% push for last. Each stage lists the trophies it earns and how far along you are.

      Platinum estimate, from PowerPyx via the guides we cite: about 4 out of 10 difficulty and roughly 200 hours. The story alone is around 50 to 60 hours.

      Missable trophies

      Only one trophy on the whole list is possibly missable. Everything else unlocks in a single playthrough or can be cleaned up after the story.

      Save habits that keep the platinum safe

      • Keep a manual save at the start of every chapter — there is no mission replay.
      • Keep one more manual save from just before the final mission.
      • Meditate at every spot you pass, including Lucia’s balcony spot in Chapter 1 — it disappears after that chapter.
      • Play with cheats off on your main save: Rockstar games have historically locked trophies while cheats are active.

      About this guide

      This guide is a static web app. It loads no network resources and sends nothing anywhere: your checklist lives in this browser’s local storage, and export writes a file you own. It works with the network off.

      Where every fact comes from

      • Trophy names, descriptions and grades — the official PlayStation 5 list, as published on Exophase and PSNProfiles, which agree on all 55.
      • Progress counts (for example, 250 stunts, 42 Shooting Range challenges) — the counts PlayStation tracks, as listed on PSNProfiles.
      • Hidden flags — which of the 55 stay hidden until earned, as listed on Exophase (28 hidden, 27 visible).
      • Roadmap order and the missable warning — built from the official descriptions, with the platinum estimate (about 4/10, roughly 200 hours) from PowerPyx, and the vault and stunt counts from the guides cited beside each trophy.
      • Guides — written in our own words for this entry. Where a place or step is not pinned down by a source, the guide says so plainly instead of inventing one. We have not played the game.

      The list at a glance

      55 trophies: 1 platinum, 2 gold, 6 silver, 46 bronze. 28 hidden. No online trophies — the whole list is single-player.
